# Korea immigration guide research · 7 November 2026

This review expands the Korea desk from three visa choices and two tasks to all **37 parent status families**, common subtypes and **21 tasks**. It does **not** establish that every current-to-target combination is eligible, or that every programme subtype has the same final document list. The interface labels a detailed checklist separately from an official route/document-category guide.

## Sources and precedence

1. Current statutes and effective programme notices control eligibility and legal duties.
2. [HiKorea’s latest status-by-status manual](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/board/BoardNtcDetailR.pt?BBS_SEQ=1&BBS_GB_CD=BS10&NTCCTT_SEQ=1062&page=1): **261106 체류민원 자격별 안내 매뉴얼.hwp**, published **6 November 2026**, reviewed **7 November 2026**. This is the source for the document categories, status chapters and common filing guidance. The notice itself says policy updates may precede manual updates and immigration can vary evidence for an individual examination.
3. [Korea Visa Portal](https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10102) supplies the consular purpose/status directory. A visa issued abroad and an in-country change/extension are separate procedures.
4. Gov24 supplies public-service routes and document baselines. Government EasyLaw explanations and Work24/EPS clarify statutory duties and employment procedures. University sources remain attributed examples for school-issued evidence; they do not establish every university’s financial threshold.

The Immigration Act edition checked is effective **2 October 2026**, Act **21857**, with `lsiSeq=290729`. The re-entry rule checked is the Enforcement Rules edition effective **23 October 2026**, Ordinance **1172**. A review date records a human/source review; it is not an automatic guarantee that no later directive exists.

The downloadable manual is linked, not republished or translated wholesale. Its notice specifies a KOGL licence with restrictions on commercial reuse/derivatives. SajiloDesk independently explains factual requirements and links the original. Research downloads and extraction files stay outside the repository and public assets. The 2023 English Visa Navigator was consulted only for background and is not treated as a current 2026 rulebook.

## Status coverage

| Group | Parent families | Important distinctions |
|---|---|---|
| Official | A-1, A-2, A-3 | Mission/agreement route; ordinary registration exemptions may apply. |
| Visa-free and short stay | B-1, B-2, C-1, C-3, C-4 | Ordinary extension/change is restricted; nationality, purpose and explicit exceptions matter. No general right to work. |
| Study, training, institutions and business | D-1 through D-10 | D-2 subtypes 1–8 are individually searchable. D-4-1 and D-4-7 are separate choices. D-10 job seeking, startup preparation and internships differ. D-7/8/9 have company/investment/trade evidence. |
| Employment | E-1 through E-10 | Profession-specific qualifications and sponsor evidence; E-8 local-government programmes, E-9 EPS and E-10 maritime routes differ. E-7-4 has a separate skilled-worker choice. |
| Family and residence | F-1 through F-6 | F-1-D has a workcation choice; F-2/F-5 subcategories require exact-route assessment. F-3 is not available through every sponsor. F-4/H-2 depend on qualifying Korean ancestry. F-5 card renewal is distinct from stay extension. |
| Other | G-1, H-1, H-2 | Particular humanitarian/medical/protection ground; nationality-specific working holiday; overseas-Korean working visit. |

Common choices appear first for the intended audience. This is an editorial convenience, not a published ranking based on user statistics. Other subcodes use their parent family and the corresponding current manual chapter. Regional, Top-Tier, K-STAR, startup and other special programmes must be checked against their own notice rather than assumed equivalent to a parent status.

| Address | [Gov24 ordinary report](https://www.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12700000026&HighCtgCD=A01010), Articles 36/98 | 15 days for registered foreigners; F-4 uses its own 14-day guide. |
| First registration | Article 31, current manual / EasyLaw | Entry timeline, identity/photo/residence and status-specific issued papers. F-4 gets a separate domestic-residence registration guide and form. |
| Passport / personal details | Articles 35/100 and EasyLaw | 15-day duty; passport issue/receipt abroad may need clock-start confirmation. |
| Workplace | Article 21, EPS/Work24, manual | E-9 employment-centre and immigration approvals; designated professional post-change report exception; H-2/E-8 differ. Students route to student-work permission. |
| Student part-time work | D-2 manual’s 시간제취업 chapter | School-signed confirmation, contract, business/employer ID, academic/language evidence; six-month waiting rule for specified subtypes; permitted hours vary. |
| Additional activity | Article 20 and relevant manual chapter | Permission before activity where required; a new main purpose may require status change instead. |
| Employment information | [MOJ 16 Dec 2025 notice](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/moj/182/601588/artclView.do) and [attachment](https://www.immigration.go.kr/bbs/moj/182/488482/download.do) | Expanded online reporting from 2 Jan 2026; paid/profit-making activities under the named D/E/F/H statuses; F-5 excluded; changed details within 15 days. |
| School / course | Article 35, Rule 49-2, manual | Transfer/report, new level and status change differ; school-age registration details may also require reporting. |
| Card | Article 33, Article 100, government registration explanation | Loss/damage/reissue vs F-5 ten-year renewal. No unverified universal 14-day card-loss claim. |
| Travel / re-entry | [Rule 44-2](https://law.go.kr/LSW/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lspttninfSeq=82628), Overseas Koreans Act | Qualifying registered residents normally one year within remaining stay; F-5 normally two years; F-4 separate law; exclusions and pending applications require care. EPS special re-entry is separate. |
| Family | F-3 and family manual chapters, receiving mission | Qualifying spouse/minor child vs visitor/other relative. E-9 is not an ordinary F-3 sponsor. |
| Child / grant | Article 23 and manual | Foreign child born in Korea: normally grant within 90 days if staying; passport, grant and registration are separate. |
| Permanent residence | F-5 manual chapter | Exact subcategory before assessing residence, income, conduct, knowledge and exemptions. No universal income/years claim. |
| Entry visa | Visa Portal and responsible mission | Consular visa form differs from in-Korea Form 34; school/employer/EPS sponsorship channels. |
| Departure | Manual and Article 37 | Final departure/card return vs temporary travel; unavoidable-departure request requires official permission. |
| Overdue | Articles 25, 94, 98, 100 | Distinguishes overstay, unauthorised work and late reports; no automatic grace period or made-up fine calculator. |
| Refusal / cancellation | Written decision and manual/help | Preserve service date, reasons and remedy instructions. A challenge is not automatically lawful continued stay. |
| Certificates | [Official local-government service explanation](https://www.dongjak.go.kr/portal/main/contents.do?menuNo=201136) and linked Gov24 | Registration, F-4 domestic residence and entry/exit certificates are distinct. |
| Application help | HiKorea forms, manual common fee table, 1345 | Booking is not filing; blank forms vs issued certificates; official fees, possible reductions/exemptions and decision tracking. |

## Exact legal numbers checked

| Duty | Deadline / limit | Authority and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary registered address change | **15 days**; criminal fine **up to ₩1,000,000** | Immigration Act **36(1)** and **98(2)**. `벌금` is not `과태료`; do not label this a fixed administrative late fee. |
| F-4 domestic address change | **14 days**; administrative fine **up to ₩2,000,000** | [Gov24 F-4 service](https://m.gov.kr/mw/AA020InfoCappView.do?CappBizCD=12700000106&HighCtgCD=A01010), Overseas Koreans Act **6(2), 17(1)** as explained by [government EasyLaw](https://easylaw.go.kr/CSP/CnpClsMainBtr.laf?ccfNo=3&cciNo=2&cnpClsNo=3&csmSeq=1136&popMenu=ov). |
| Registered-detail changes | **15 days**; administrative fine **up to ₩1,000,000** | Immigration Act **35**, **100(2)(1)**. Covered changes and clock-start exceptions still need correct context. |
| First registration | Normally **90 days** from entry; up to **one year imprisonment or ₩10,000,000 criminal fine** for the statutory registration violation | Articles **31, 95(7)**; exemptions and grant/change-time registration requirements apply. |
| F-5 physical card | **10 years**; renew before card expiry; administrative fine **up to ₩2,000,000** | Articles **33(3–4), 100(1)(4)**. Permanent status does not have an ordinary stay-extension application. |
| E-9 workplace change | Normally apply within **one month** after contract ends and obtain immigration permission within **three months** of applying | Employment Permits Act **25**, current manual and Work24. Statutory exceptions, reasons, permitted changes and existing stay expiry remain relevant. |
| Newborn foreign child | Normally **90 days** from birth | Article **23**; separately confirm documents for departure within that period. |

Every penalty amount is a **statutory maximum for the stated violation**, not a prediction, fee quote or promise of an immigration outcome. The guides do not publish a guessed day-by-day fine schedule.

- Review dates are explicit per guide, displayed outside collapsed details and included in downloaded lists. Source publication/effective dates are distinct from SajiloDesk’s review date.
- The existing 90-day stale-review notice remains. No automatic source-monitoring service has been created.
- If an official source changes, recheck the precise subcategory, form, deadline, legal penalty type, effective date and source link. Update the guide date only after review.
- Preserve old guide URLs and comment threads. New guides join the same private moderation workflow; no reader experience becomes an official requirement without source verification.
- Exact nationality, income/points, regional quota, consular jurisdiction and individual case decisions remain outside automatic eligibility determination. The route labels make those boundaries visible instead of presenting an indiscriminate universal checklist.
