# SajiloDesk / सजिलो डेस्क

**Everyday tasks, made sajilo.**

Free everyday tools for Nepal, Nepalis, and beyond.

**[Open SajiloDesk in English](https://sajilodesk.com/en/)** · **[नेपालीमा खोल्नुहोस्](https://sajilodesk.com/ne/)**

## Everyday tools, ready when you are

| Tool | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| BS ↔ AD date converter | Switch a date between Nepali and English calendars. |
| Age calculator | Find your age today or on an application deadline. |
| Preeti to Unicode | Turn legacy Preeti text into readable Nepali Unicode. |
| Photo resize & compress | Adjust photo dimensions, format and file size, then download a copy. |
| Everyday PDF tools | Preview every page, extract or merge pages, compress PDFs or save a page as JPG/PNG. |
| Land-area converter | Convert ropani, aana, bigha, kattha, square feet and square metres. |

Each tool opens with a ready-to-try example. Replace the values or choose your own photo or PDF to get started.

## Life abroad · Korea

Visa paperwork in Korea? [Find your guide](https://sajilodesk.com/en/life-abroad/) by choosing your visa and what you want to do. Search by typing a code such as **D2**. Find help with renewals, visa changes, registration, work, family and travel, with Korean document names, official sources and a checked date. Save or download your preparation list. Available in Nepali and English.

Share an experience, suggest a missing document or request a new guide in the comments at the end. Comments are reviewed before publication; no sign-up is needed. Check your individual requirements with immigration and your school or employer.

## Your day, at a glance

See today's Nepali and English dates, Nepal and world key days, and listed holidays. Browse a [BS/AD calendar](https://sajilodesk.com/en/#calendar), select a date and explore its details. Holiday dates appear in red, with regional or community scope explained alongside them.

## Simple and private

- Free to use, with no account required.
- Available in English and नेपाली.
- Photos, PDFs, text and calculations are processed in your browser.
- Save favourite tools on your own device for quick access.

SajiloDesk is an independent utility site. Check the issuing authority's rules when preparing official applications. The holiday list currently covers BS 2083; sources and coverage are available in the site.
