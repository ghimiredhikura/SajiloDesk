# Korea eligibility review — 7 November 2026

Scope: Nepali citizens in Korea. This review supersedes the earlier “all 37 families” public chooser. The catalogue now has 113 choices across 32 parent families, including F-5 and 29 separate permanent-residence routes. It does not claim every internal immigration subcode is documented. Unlisted specialist programmes remain under the parent family and official manual.

## Other checks

- **F-2-99:** current manual’s long-term-resident section requires applicant assets of at least ₩20 million **and** prior-year applicant income of at least ₩40 million; a stricter current-status maintenance rule prevails. This is not an automatic GNI rule. Knowledge alternatives include KIIP 4, pre-assessment 81+, prescribed Korean education or exemption.
- **F-2-7:** eligible applicant class plus at least 80 points; duration exceptions mean “three years for everyone” is incorrect.
- **F-2-7S:** use the current K-STAR designated-university and recommendation provisions, including the explicitly covered integrated/doctoral study stages.
- **Top-Tier:** separately checked ordinary F-2-T 3× and E-7-T 2× GNI routes, education/career/recommendation conditions, and F-2-T’s prescribed alternatives. High income alone is insufficient.
- **F-1-D:** F-1 §13 has age/region-based 1×/1.5×/2× GNI rules, a distinct accompanying-family exception, one year of same-industry experience and ₩100 million private medical/repatriation cover. Do not retain a blanket 2× rule.
- **D-8/D-10/E-7/F-6/C-3/G-1:** subcodes checked in their manual sections. Family summaries deliberately avoid inventing universal salary, bank-balance, age or quota figures where the exact programme/occupation/year matters.

## Applicability limits and future updates

The manual says previous-year GNI (or the earlier year if not announced), but the public evidence reviewed did **not** establish when the June statistical revision becomes the F-5 filing cutoff. Both official releases and their dates are therefore shown. The site does not claim an unverified April-to-March validity period. Ask 1345/the receiving office which announced figure it applies. This is a specific unresolved implementation point, not a claim that the published statistics are uncertain.

F-5 income ordinarily uses the previous calendar tax year, not a rolling 365 days. The earlier tax year may apply when the relevant tax certificate is not yet issuable. General pooling/50%-own-share rules have exceptions; F-5-10 and F-5-13 have applicant-only rules. Assets must meet the permitted route, debt, holding and maintenance tests; income and assets cannot be summed.

F-4/H-2, Korean-ancestry F-5-6/7/14 and the historical ethnic-Chinese F-5-8 route are excluded from the chooser. B-1/B-2 are not presented as ordinary Nepali passport routes. Legacy published URLs and comment-thread identifiers are retained with a scope notice. Known mismatches are removed from the target menu; unusual historical/individual exceptions remain reachable through “Other / special-case route”.
