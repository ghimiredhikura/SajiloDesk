# Sajilo Desk: evidence and opportunity backlog

Research date: **4 November 2026**. Audience: Nepal first, then Nepalis abroad and people handling Nepal-related documents in English. This is a desk-research snapshot, not a claim of user interviews, measured conversion uplift, or guaranteed search rankings.

The strongest starting proposition is a small, dependable workbench for **dates, application age, legacy text, photos and land units**. Give each task a direct bilingual page, a visible method, and a usable copy/download result. Connect related tasks without retaining personal inputs. The five-tool first release remains the priority; broader information feeds would add continuing editorial work.

## What the evidence supports

Its proposed thresholds are decision criteria, not results. Its paid-tool suggestion is superseded by the current brief: core services remain free. These are official-site content inspections on 4 November 2026. A listed feature is a provider claim unless noted otherwise. **No competitor mobile interaction benchmark was completed:** tap counts, errors, speed, ad interference, upload limits and output quality remain unmeasured. Do not use this table to claim Sajilo Desk is faster or more accurate.

| Nagarik Sathi | [Official site](https://nagarik.nexenture.com.np/) offers EN/नेपाली controls, service search, guides and official-service handoff; states independence from government | Service guidance and responsibility are clearly separated | Specific office coverage, review dates and a household's understanding of the handoff |

The differentiation is therefore a **hypothesis**: clear conversion conventions, consistent Nepali/English controls, helpful error recovery, and a short sequence that shops can repeat. Local processing and connected tools alone are not novel. Avoid unsupported “best”, “most accurate”, “government-approved” or adoption claims.

## Important source findings and publication boundaries

- **Passport size conflict persists.** On 4 November, the [domestic page](https://nepalpassport.gov.np/en/process/process-23) says scan originals at 300 KB or less, while the [general process](https://nepalpassport.gov.np/en/process/process-40) says 1 MB or less. The live application route was not traversed. No government-specific preset or complete verified passport checklist should be inferred from this research.
- **International reach should start with Nepal-related tasks.** English BS↔AD output and a future applicant-specific police-certificate guide offer a concrete audience. Generic image/PDF utilities compete with established global products; use them to complete the Nepal workflow, then test whether standalone global demand appears.
- **Daily data is a separate product commitment.** AMPIS shows dated wholesale, market-specific data. Publication on an official website does not establish permission for automated redistribution or guarantee a maintained API. The phase-one site should not display invented “today” market/forex/gold values.
- **Mobile-first is a design choice to validate.** The [NSO/UNICEF survey landing page](https://www.unicef.org/nepal/reports/multiple-indicator-cluster-survey-2024-25) confirms the 2024–25 survey provenance. This pass did not independently validate the PDF's detailed device percentages; they should not appear as new measured findings.
- **Land source verified during implementation.** After the initial research fetch timed out, the government-hosted Construction Handbook was downloaded and PDF page 16 was rendered and visually inspected. Its square-foot factors and compound ratios match the implementation. Metric approximations in the source are not treated as exact values; the calculator uses the exact international-foot factor. See [the validation ledger](validation-ledger.md).
